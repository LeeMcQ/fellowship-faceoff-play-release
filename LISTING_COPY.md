## Full description — max 4000

```
Fellowship Face-Off is a Christian party game for church groups, youth nights, and families.

Pass one phone around the room. Teams Sing, Act, or Explain biblical words — then try Championship mode or browse the Library. Optional denomination word packs let your group use language that fits your tradition.

Works offline after the first load. Built for Friday night fellowship, youth camps, small groups, and family game night.

GAME MODES
• Sing — hint the word through song (no full lyrics spoiling required; have fun with it)
• Act — mime the word without speaking
• Explain — describe the word without saying it
• Championship — competitive rounds for the whole group
• Library — browse and learn the word list at your own pace

WHAT YOU GET
• Hundreds of Bible-themed words for church party play
• Optional denomination packs
• Offline play after first visit
• Phone-friendly dark UI for dim rooms and projector-free nights
• No account required to start a game

ABOUT THIS ANDROID APP
This listing is a Trusted Web Activity (TWA) that opens the Fellowship Face-Off progressive web app:
https://leemcq.github.io/logosliving/

Content and game updates can arrive with the website. Package name: com.leemcq.fellowshipfaceoff

Privacy policy: https://leemcq.github.io/logosliving/privacy.html
```
