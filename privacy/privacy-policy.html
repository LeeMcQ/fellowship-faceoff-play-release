Privacy Policy for Fellowship Face-Off

Effective date: 15 September 2026 · Developer: Lee McQuire · App / site: Fellowship Face-Off

This policy explains what Fellowship Face-Off (“the App”) does with information when you use the website at https://leemcq.github.io/logosliving/ or an Android app that opens that experience (including a Trusted Web Activity).

1. What the App is

Fellowship Face-Off is a Christian party game for church groups, youth nights, and families. You can play Sing, Act, Explain, Championship, and Library modes, including optional denomination word packs.

2. Information stored on your device

The App stores game-related data locally on your device (for example via browser local storage), such as:

  • Unlock or Full Edition status and denomination-pack unlock flags
  • Words you have already used in a session or week
  • Simple preferences or stats needed to run the game

This on-device data is not a cloud account. Clearing site data / app storage resets it.

3. Information you choose to share

If you use Share, WhatsApp, or ministry / unlock request flows, you may send information (such as your name, church, location, email, or WhatsApp number, and a message) using your device’s share sheet or messaging apps. That content goes to the people or channels you choose. We only see it if you send it to us.

4. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the App may remind you about game nights or updates. You can deny or revoke notification permission in system or browser settings.

5. Advertising

The free web experience may show ads through advertising partners (for example NitroPay / related Google ad infrastructure) when ads are enabled. Ad partners may collect device and usage identifiers according to their own policies, to show and measure ads. Paid / Full Edition users should not see those ads when unlock is active.

Play Store builds: advertising and any advertising ID usage will be declared in the Google Play Data safety form for that build. If a Play build ships without ads, this section does not apply to that build.

6. Fonts and network services

The App may load fonts or scripts from third-party networks (for example Google Fonts) when online. Those providers may receive standard technical data such as IP address in normal HTTPS requests.

7. Children

The App is intended for church and family gatherings and is not directed at children under 13 as a primary audience. Parents and leaders should supervise younger players. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child provided personal details to us directly, contact us and we will delete them.

8. Payments and unlocks

Digital unlocks may be offered via codes or, in an Android Play build, via Google Play Billing. Payment details for Play purchases are processed by Google, not by us. Do not send card numbers to us over WhatsApp.

9. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with processors needed to run ads (if enabled), to receive messages you send us, or when required by law.

10. Retention and deletion

On-device data remains until you clear it. Messages you send us are kept only as long as needed to respond to your request. To ask for deletion of information you sent us, email the address below.

11. Security

We use HTTPS for the hosted App. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top will change when we do. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
